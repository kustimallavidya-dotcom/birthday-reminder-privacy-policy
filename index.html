Privacy Policy — Birthday Reminder

Effective Date: 27 September 2026

App Name: Birthday Reminder

Developer: MilindSoftLabs

Package ID: com.milindsoftlabs.birthdayreminder

1. Introduction

Birthday Reminder is a personal birthday reminder application developed by MilindSoftLabs. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information when you use Birthday Reminder.

2. Information You Enter in the App

Birthday Reminder allows you to save information such as:

  • Person's name
  • Date of birth
  • Designation or relationship
  • Mobile phone number, if you choose to provide one

This information is used only to provide the app's birthday reminder and related features.

3. How Your Information Is Used

The information saved in the app may be used to:

  • Display saved birthdays
  • Calculate and display upcoming birthdays
  • Calculate and display age
  • Generate birthday reminders and notifications
  • Display the person's name in birthday notifications
  • Provide birthday greeting card and greeting message features
  • Open WhatsApp when you choose to send a birthday wish using a saved mobile number

4. Local Storage and Offline Operation

Birthday Reminder is designed to work offline.

The birthday information you enter is stored locally on your device for the app's functionality. The app does not require an internet connection to provide its core birthday reminder features.

5. Personal Information Sharing

MilindSoftLabs does not sell or rent the information entered into Birthday Reminder.

The app does not provide an account or sign-in system, and it does not require you to create an account to use its core features.

When you choose to use the WhatsApp action, the app may open WhatsApp on your device so that you can send a message. The sending of that message is handled by WhatsApp and is subject to WhatsApp's own terms and privacy practices.

6. Notifications

Birthday Reminder may use Android notifications to remind you about birthdays.

Notifications may display information such as the person's name and birthday-related message.

You can manage notification permissions through your Android device settings.

7. Internet Access

The app's core functionality does not require an internet connection.

8. Data Security

The information stored by the app is intended for your personal use on your device.

You should protect your device using appropriate security measures such as a screen lock or other device security features.

9. Children's Privacy

Birthday Reminder is a general-purpose personal utility application. It is not specifically directed toward children.

The app does not knowingly request children to provide personal information.

10. Third-Party Services

Birthday Reminder does not require third-party online services for its core birthday reminder functionality.

If you choose to open WhatsApp from the app, WhatsApp operates according to its own terms and privacy policy.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the app or applicable requirements.

Any updated version will be made available through the location where this Privacy Policy is published.
